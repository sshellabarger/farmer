## What changed

**X1 - trusted proxies** (`src/utils/trusted-proxies.ts`, `src/app.ts`). `trustProxy: true` is replaced by an explicit list: loopback (the function hands requests to `fastify.inject`) plus the twenty Google service ranges. `request.ip` is now the first untrusted address from the right of `X-Forwarded-For`: the true client through Hosting (`<client>, <google proxy>`) and on the direct function URL (`<anything>, <client>`). Tests go through `buildApp().inject`: the limiter and the stored `ip` / `sms_consent.ip` / `signature.ip` use the real address; rotating a spoofed first hop still reaches 429 on `POST /api/applications` and `POST /api/applications/uploads`; no header still reads `127.0.0.1`.

**X2 - uploads and submissions cannot be flooded** (`src/services/application-uploads.ts`, `src/routes/applications.ts`, `src/functions.ts`, web `api-apply.ts` + `upload-field.tsx`). The upload takes a `market` form field and is refused unless that market exists, is active and its application window is open (404 / 409). A global cap of 300 upload documents per UTC day (`day` field, one equality query) and a per-market cap of 150 submissions per UTC day both answer 503 with a plain message and alert staff through `notifyError` (throttled by signature). `purgeUnattachedUploads` runs inside the nightly `rollMarketDates` in its own try/catch: it deletes the object and the document of every upload with no `application_id` older than 48 hours; one failed delete is counted and the run carries on.

**X2/X3 follow-up found on review** (`c891674`). The two first commits interacted badly: a submission is two writes (the application, then `application_id` on each upload); a request that died in between left an application whose files read "unattached"; the idempotent repeat (X3a) answered early and never marked them; the purge would have deleted them after 48 hours. Now the purge asks, before deleting, whether an application of the same draft lists the upload in `upload_ids` and, if so, marks it attached and keeps it (`reattached` in the result and the log line); and every repeat of an accepted submission re-runs the idempotent `attachUploads`.

**X3 - submission correctness** (`src/routes/applications.ts`, `src/services/applications.ts`, `src/db/firestore.ts`). (a) A repeat of an accepted submission (same draft and email) gets the original 201 also when it referenced uploads. A second pass on review moved that check to the top of the handler: the repeat is answered before anything that may have changed since the submission was accepted (the window closing at midnight, a new rules or consent version, a retired stall), so a phone that retries the request never tells an accepted applicant they were refused. (b) The public duplicate answer is still 409 and no longer carries the other application's id. (c) `sms_consent_version` must equal the market's `legal.sms_consent_version` when that is set (409, `code: sms_consent_changed`). (d) One live application per email, market and season is enforced by `create()` on `application_keys/<market>_<season>_<sha256(email)>` before the application; a declined or withdrawn holder lets the next one take the key. (e) Approval takes `workflow_locks/application_approve__<id>` (and `producer_identity__<sha256(email)>` while the producer is resolved) with `create()`; a fresh lock held by someone else is 409 "Approval already in progress"; a stale one is taken over after the engine's `CLAIM_TTL_MS`.

**X4 - rules route** (`src/routes/markets.ts`, `src/services/markets.ts`). `GET /api/markets/:id/rules` tests the id against `MARKET_SLUG_RE` before Firestore is asked (an encoded slash used to become a 500 and an alert on the real SDK) and answers 404 for an inactive market. `MARKET_SLUG_RE` is the one pattern; `MARKET_ID_RE` in `applications.ts` is an alias of it.

**X5 - staff-triggered texts respect quiet hours and the application window** (`src/services/invites.ts`, `src/services/markets.ts` `quietUntil`, `src/services/agreements.ts`, routes `producers`, `markets`, `insurance`, `applications`; admin pages).
- Invites (`POST /api/producers/:id/invite`, `POST /api/markets/:id/invite-reapply`) answer 409 `code: applications_not_open` unless the window is open today in the market's calendar. A dry run of the bulk invite still answers and reports `applications_open`.
- Inside the market's quiet hours the email goes and the text does not: `sms_status: 'skipped_quiet_hours'`, with `quiet_until` in the response.
- The invite record tracks the channels apart: `email_status` / `email_sent_at`, `sms_status` / `sms_sent_at`, `completions[]`; a channel reads `pending` between the `create()` of the record and its send. Re-running an invite without `force` sends only what is missing, with the same token and the same dedupe key `application_invite:<market>:<season>:<producer>`, so the re-run after quiet hours texts exactly the producers not texted yet, and a producer who gained a phone (or an email) since the first invite gets it. The bulk result adds `completed`, `held_quiet_hours`, `quiet_until`.
- Approve with send during quiet hours: email sent, `sends.sms: 'skipped_quiet_hours'`, `sends.quiet_until`. `POST /api/memberships/:id/agreement-link {send:true}` answers 409 `code: quiet_hours` before a token is minted; `{send:false}` still returns the link. `issueAgreementLink` carries the same guard.

**X6 - email** (`src/utils/html.ts`, `src/services/email.ts`, `src/services/invites.ts`). The wrapper header is "SJCA Markets" and the footer "St. Joseph Center of Arkansas - SJCA Markets"; the subject is escaped where the page repeats it. `escapeHtml` and `htmlLink` are new; the invitation and acceptance bodies escape every interpolated value (the applicant-typed first name first of all) and render each address as a link. ## Residuals (accepted, not fixed here)

- **Trusted proxies.** A caller able to send from an address inside Google's service ranges (for example Apps Script) reaching the direct function URL can still pose as the proxy; the daily caps are the backstop. If Google fronts Hosting from a range that is not listed, Hosting traffic is keyed per proxy address (early 429s, evidence addresses that all belong to Google) until `src/utils/trusted-proxies.ts` is updated.
- **Daily caps are backstops, not ledgers.** Requests that overlap can each pass the count, so a day can end a few documents over a cap.
- **Key and lock takeovers are read-then-set** (`claimApplicationKey`, `acquireStaffLock`), the same documented residual as the engine's `claim()`: two requests arriving together at a key that is already free to take can both proceed. Worst case: two applications from one address in the queue, the second approval refused.
- **Two new applicants with different emails and the same mobile number, approved at the same instant**, could both pass the phone-conflict read. The identity lock is per email.
- **A text the provider failed is not retried by a plain re-run of an invite**: its `messages` row holds the dedupe key. The admin page offers "Resend the invitation" (force).
- **A forced resend made during quiet hours** re-sends the email and holds the text; the later plain re-run then reports `duplicate` if the first invite's text had gone out (that producer was texted, with the earlier link, which still works).
- **Completing an invite is not locked**: two admins re-running at the same instant can both re-send a previously failed email (never the text, which is keyed).
- **Staff emails still interpolate unescaped values.** `buildDeadlineSummaryEmail` in `src/services/checkin-workflow.ts` (fixer Y's file) puts producer business names into HTML sent to `ALERT_EMAIL`, and business names are applicant-typed since Phase 4; `error-notify.ts` and `support-notify.ts` do the same with error text and feedback text. Staff-only recipients. `escapeHtml` in `src/utils/html.ts` is there to use.
- **`logo` uploads** are accepted by the upload route but no form submits one and no application names one, so they are purged after 48 hours.
- **A non-ASCII market name** (outside the GSM alphabet) drops SMS segments to 67 characters; the acceptance text then cannot fit two segments and is sent with its full wording.
- **Inside quiet hours an invite to a producer with a phone and no email sends nothing at all** (the record says `skipped_no_email` / `skipped_quiet_hours`); the re-run after quiet hours sends the text.
