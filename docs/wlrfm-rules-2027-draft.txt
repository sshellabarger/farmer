WEST LITTLE ROCK FARMERS MARKET
Rules and Regulations
2027 Season
THE 2027 WEST LITTLE ROCK FARMERS MARKET AT BRECKENRIDGE VILLAGE WILL RUN EVERY SATURDAY FROM 8AM – 12NOON FROM APRIL 3, THROUGH NOVEMBER 27, 2027, FOR A TOTAL OF 35 MARKETS.
SECTION 1. Market Mission
The West Little Rock Farmers Market exists to support Arkansas farmers, food producers, and artisans by providing a vibrant, well-managed marketplace where all products are grown, raised, or crafted in Arkansas. Our mission is to strengthen Central Arkansas’s local food economy, create consistent and reliable sales opportunities for local producers, and build a welcoming community space that celebrates Arkansas’s food culture, gardening traditions, and strong sense of local community.
SECTION 2. Governance & Authority
The Market is managed by the St. Joseph's Center of Arkansas staff, as part of the WLRFM Advisory Board, a subcommittee of the Arkansas Local Food Network. Market Manager responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
- Oversee day-to-day operations
- Handle vendor disputes
- Enforce all Market rules and regulations
The Advisory Board serves as the final authority on appeals or disputes beyond the Market Manager’s purview.
SECTION 3. Vendor Eligibility & Application
Eligibility
- Vendors must fall into one or more of the approved Vendor Categories.
- Farmers/Ranchers: fruits, vegetables, meats, mushrooms, nuts, seeds, grains, eggs, herbs, plants, flowers etc.
- Specialty Food: baked goods, mixes, jams/jellies, honey, condiments, beverages, cheese, pet items, etc.
- Food adjacent products: such as woodworkers, potters, fabric crafts, plant/botanical arts, natural healing/skincare/cleaning products
- Crafts: non-food products such as soaps, lotions, candles, clothing, pottery, artwork, jewelry, leather, etc, may be added for special events
A healthy balance among these categories will be maintained at the discretion of the Market Manager and Advisory Board, with an emphasis on Farmers/Ranchers and Specialty Food producers.
- Vendors must grow, raise, or produce ALL the items they sell, and all items MUST be listed on application. No sales of any non-approved items. Mid season changes MUST be approved by the market manager prior to offering new items for sale.
- Vendors must hold any required permits relevant to their product type and accurately represent production methods and certifications.
- Vendors must agree to complete a market report each week to assist the market manager in identifying gaps in product availability and other pertinent data.
- Vendors agree to random farm or facility visits to verify product origin, as initiated by the Market Manager, the Advisory Board, or their designated appointee(s).
Insurance Requirements:
- All vendors must carry a minimum of $1,000,000 in general liability insurance
- Proof of insurance is required annually and prior to market participation
- Vendors must maintain coverage for the entire season and name (shopping center owners) KBK-Breck, The WLRFM and ALFN as additional insured
- Vendors must sign the official Hold Harmless Agreement once their application is accepted.
- Vendors who do not currently have insurance may request guidance from the Market Manager
Liability
Vendors are solely responsible for damages or injuries caused by their products that are unsafe, adulterated, mislabeled, or misrepresented
Application Process
- Vendors must submit an annual application and sign the acknowledgment of these Rules and Regulations, and applications must be approved prior to participation. Submission of application does not guarantee acceptance.
- Vendors must submit proof of liability insurance by the first market day. Additional information about market liability insurance can be found HERE.
- New vendors may apply mid-season with:
- Approval by the Market Manager
- Alignment with Market product needs
- New vendors may be subject to a trial period
Acceptance is based on:
- Product balance across vendor categories
- Compliance with rules
- Available space

SECTION 7. Compliance with Laws & Regulations
Vendors Must:
- Follow all applicable laws and regulations, including:
- Arkansas Department of Health (ADH) food safety regulations
- Arkansas Livestock and Poultry Commission requirements for eggs, poultry, and meat
- USDA/NOP organic standards (if applicable)
- Obtain and maintain all necessary permits and licenses
- Handle all sales tax collection and reporting independently
- Vendors must practice good personal hygiene when sampling and wash hands or utilize gloves to reduce cross-contamination. If a product requires cold storage, vendors must store samples safely
Food Safety, Permits, & Licenses
General Rule
- Vendors must comply with all Arkansas Department of Health (ADH) regulations and guidelines. Vendors must maintain necessary permits and licenses before and during participation in the market.
- For detailed requirements refer to the Farmers Market Vendor Guidelines here.
No Permit Required
- Whole, uncut produce
- Properly labeled Food Freedom products (see Guidelines here)
- Honey and sorghum
- Microgreens
Permit Required
- Canned goods not approved by the Food Freedom Act
- Dairy products
- TCS (Time/Temperature Control for Safety) foods
Prohibited Items
- Wild-harvested mushrooms
- Sprouts (microgreens are allowed)
Food Handling/Safety Practices
- Follow ADH guidelines for sampling.
- Maintain proper temperature and sanitary food storage.
- No unapproved health claims on herbal products which must comply with FDA/FTC guidance
Eggs, Meat & Poultry Guidelines
Eggs
- Must be washed, pre-packaged, and labeled “ungraded.”
- Labels must include farm name and address.
- Must be refrigerated at all times.
Meat & Poultry
- Must be processed in a USDA-inspected facility and sold frozen.
- Labels must include:
  - Product name
  - Net Weight
- Farm name and address
Plants & Nursery Products
Vendors must comply with Arkansas State Plant Board regulations.

POLICIES AND PROCEDURES
Inclement Weather
The West Little Rock Farmers Market operates rain or shine. The market will not be cancelled due to rain, heat, or general inclement weather. The market will only be cancelled or suspended under the following conditions: a Tornado Watch, Tornado Warning, or SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WARNING issued by the National Weather service and is in effect prior to or during the market operating hours.
During Market operations:
- Market management will monitor weather alerts and notify vendors and guests immediately
- All vendors and guests will be directed to take shelter in the Film Alley building
- Vendors should secure their tents, displays, and merchandise to the extent safely possible before taking shelter. Unsecured tents, canopies, etc can become dangerous projectiles in high winds.
- At the discretion of the management, the market may resume operations if the watch or warning is lifted.
Market management will communicate, via all channels, any cancellation or delay of opening as early as possible.
Emergency Response
In the event of a medical emergency, fire, or other urgent situation:
- Call 911 Immediately
- Notify Market Managers as soon as possible
- Direct First Responders to the front of the Film Alley building, the designated area – or to the location of an injured or ill person. Provide assistance if trained, but do not move an injured or ill person unless they are in immediate danger.
- Keep the area clear
A first aid kit is available at the market information booth at all times.
All vendors should familiarize themselves with the Film Alley facility as well as all entrances and exits to the market area.
Active Threat/Active Shooter Response
Safety of vendors, customers and staff is our highest priority! In this unlikely event, adopt the RUN HIDE FIGHT Protocol:
RUN – if you can safely escape, do so
- If you can help others, do, but do not let others slow you down
- Move away from the source of the threat
- Call 911 as soon as you are safe
HIDE – if you cannot escape
- Hide in the Film Alley building or another nearby structure
- Lock doors/barricades
- Silence your phone
- Remain hidden and quiet
FIGHT – the last resort
- Act aggressively and use anything available to defend yourself
- Work with others if possible
When Law Enforcement arrives
- Remain calm and follow instructions
- Keep hands visible and empty
- Do not point, scream or make sudden movements
- Do provide any information about the threat when asked
The Film Alley building is the designated Emergency location. The building will be used as the reunification and accountability point once the scene has been declared safe by law enforcement.
